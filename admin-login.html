Delhi Civic Administration Rule 4(A)

Under the Delhi Municipal Jurisdiction Framework:

  • MCD Officers cannot inspect, modify, or close PWD, DJB, or DDA tickets.
  • PWD Officers have exclusive authority over highways (≥60ft) and cannot enter MCD colony systems.
  • DJB Officers oversee water/sewerage grids and cannot access road asphalt depots.
  • DDA Officers are restricted strictly to designated park perimeters and urban greens.
  • Central Master Room is reserved exclusively for the Chief Secretary (DELHI-CHIEF-01).
